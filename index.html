Control whether other people can see when you are active on Ghost.

Show my active statusWhen this is on, a green dot shows on your profile while you are active, you appear in your friends' "Active now" list, and chats show when you were last active. Turn it off and nobody can see your activity anywhere — no dot, no list entry, no last-active time.

Brand Account

Made for people with a big audience. Convert to a Brand Account and your Messages split in two, so the people closest to you never get buried under thousands of follower messages.

  • Personal — chats with people you follow and who follow you back. Your close circle, always separate.
  • Brand — everyone else: followers you haven't followed back, and anyone new who messages you.
  • Chats move automatically: when someone you follow follows you back, their chat moves to Personal. If either of you unfollows, it moves back to Brand.
